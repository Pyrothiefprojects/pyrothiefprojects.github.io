Overview

Drift Remote does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. This policy describes what the app does and does not do with information on your device.

Data Collection

Drift Remote does not collect any data. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party SDKs are included in the app.

Local Storage

The app stores preferences locally on your device using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults). This includes settings such as haptics preferences and display options. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to Pyrothief Projects or any third party.

Bluetooth

Drift Remote uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to communicate directly with nearby Apple devices. Device names and identifiers are used only to establish and maintain the connection and are never transmitted off your device or stored beyond the current session.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach Pyrothief Projects via GitHub at github.com/Pyrothiefprojects.
